Nepal Community Saving & Credit Cooperative Society (SACCOS) is a member-based cooperative established on July 11, 1999. We merged with 4 other SACCOS on January 3, 2013.

Our Foundation

Registered under the Co-operative Act 2048 BS, we provide financial services to improve the economic and social life of our members.

  • Established: July 11, 1999
  • Members: 15,000+
  • Service Centers: 12
  • Total Savings: Rs. 2.5B
