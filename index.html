Nepal Community SACCOS

Nepal Community Saving & Credit Cooperative Society is a member-based cooperative established on July 11, 1999. We merged with 4 other SACCOS on January 3, 2013, creating a stronger institution to serve our community.

Registered under the Co-operative Act 2048 BS, we work to improve the economic and social life of our members. As a democratic institution, every member has equal rights regardless of shareholding.

We provide affordable financial services including savings, loans, insurance, and remittance to empower our community economically.
